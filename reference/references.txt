1. Sidi, M. J. (1997). Spacecraft Dynamics and Control: A Practical Engineering Approach. Cambridge University Press. 
   - Explains the rigid body dynamics of satellites and the design of PID controllers for attitude control.

2. Song, Y. et al. "Pulse-width pulse-frequency modulator for satellite attitude control." IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems.
   - Provides standard formulations and tuning techniques for the PWPFM (Pulse-Width Pulse-Frequency Modulator), consisting of a first-order lag filter and a Schmitt trigger.

3. Krovel, T. D. (2005). "Optimal tuning of PWPF modulator for attitude control." Norwegian University of Science and Technology.
   - Typical values for Schmitt trigger thresholds: U_on = 0.45 U_m, U_off = 0.15 U_m.

4. Open literature parameters used for simulation:
   - Bandwidth = 0.05 Hz
   - Damping ratio = 0.9
   - Computational Delay = 16 ms
   - RCS System Delay = 64 ms
